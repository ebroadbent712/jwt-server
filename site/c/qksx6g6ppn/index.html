10 shortcuts to family photos that look effortless

Outfit
Cheat Codes

Figuring out what to wear is the worst. These make it easier. They're cheat codes, not commandments: break any of them on purpose.

Use them, skip them, or send them to the person in charge of outfits.

Cheat code 01

Crown one star.

One person wears the standout piece, usually a print and usually Mom. Everyone else wears quieter colors pulled from it, so the star can shine.

How to say it“Let's make your floral dress the star and pull everyone else's colors from it.”

Cheat code 02

Pick 3–5 colors and stay in your lane.

Every piece lives inside one palette. Neutral shoes and belts are free.

How to say it“Here's our palette: dusty rose, olive, cream and greige. Anything in these works.”

Cheat code 03

One print. Two, tops.

Two prints should share a color and be different sizes. Three is a circus.

How to say it“Let's keep the floral on you and give Ella the tartan skirt. Everyone else stays solid.”

Cheat code 04

Coordinate, don't match.

Four people in white tees and jeans is a boy band reunion. Same palette, different pieces.

How to say it“Think same family, different outfits. A playlist, not a uniform.”

Cheat code 05

Spread out the darks.

Mix light and dark across parents and kids so nobody disappears into a black hole.

How to say it“Dad's in navy, so let's keep you lighter and put a little navy on the kids.”

Cheat code 06

Texture does the heavy lifting.

Knits, corduroy, tweed, fair isle. A cardigan or jacket on half the crew adds instant depth.

How to say it“Swap the cotton shirt for a sweater. Texture is what makes it look expensive.”

Cheat code 07

Same dress level for everyone.

Nobody in a gown next to a hoodie. Keep everyone within one step of each other.

How to say it“We're going dressy casual: a nice sweater, not a suit.”

Cheat code 08

Dress for the place.

Green vanishes in a leafy summer park and brick red vanishes downtown. Pick colors that stand out from where you're standing. Under 55°F, the coats are part of the outfit.

How to say it“Since we're at the park in summer, let's skip green and go warm: rust, cream and navy will pop.”

Cheat code 09

Comfort is a cheat code.

A toddler in itchy tights gives you exactly zero smiles. Soft, stretchy, and room to sit on the ground.

How to say it“Pick what they'd happily wear to the park. Happy kids photograph better than fancy ones.”

Cheat code 10

Let the reluctant one off easy.

Dad hates dressing up? Dark jeans, a sweater in a palette color, his own sneakers. The rest of the family carries the look.

How to say it“He can wear his jeans. We'll just pick the sweater so it ties in.”

Bonus

Leave these at home

  • Logos and words
  • Neon (it casts color onto skin)
  • Tiny, high-contrast stripes
  • Shiny or metallic fabric
  • Character prints
  • Bright white head to toe (try ivory)
