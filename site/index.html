Who made this

Hi, I'm Emily.

I've photographed families for over a decade, and every session starts with the same question: "So… what should we wear?"

I've watched moms find the photographer, pick the date, wrangle six schedules, and then get stuck dressing everyone. For years my answer was a style guide. Families skimmed page one, then texted me outfit photos at 11pm the night before. I'm a mom too, so I get it.

So I took everything I know about dressing families for photos and turned it into one plan: made for your family, with close matches you can buy today, and easy to change until photo day.

You just wear this.

Questions

Good questions. Short answers.

How much is it?

$19 for one family plan, with unlimited swaps until photo day.

Can I buy it now?

Not yet. I'm finishing a small beta with real families first. Join the first-access list and I'll email you when it opens.

How fast do I get my plan?

Usually about a minute after you finish the questions. You get a link you can open on any phone, and a copy by email.

Do I have to buy everything?

No. Mark what you already own and shop only the gaps. Each piece has up to three price points.

Are the links the exact pieces in the pictures?

The pictures show the look. Each link goes to a real piece from a real store that closely matches it, at up to three prices, so you can pick what suits your budget and sizes. Plain store links, no affiliate tracking.

What if something sells out or doesn't fit?

Swap it, and the rest of the plan still works together. Your link updates.

Who can it plan for?

Up to eight people in one plan, from babies to grandparents.
