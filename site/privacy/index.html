Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This explains what we collect when you get a family outfit plan at justwearthis.co, and what we do with it. Just Wear This is run by EB Photography, LLC.

What we collect

  • Your first name, family last name (optional) and email address.
  • What you tell us about your family and your photos: first names, age groups, which department each person shops from, what they won't wear, your photo date and setting, color and print preferences, pieces you already own, and any notes you add.
  • Payment details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

We don't ask for photos of people, sizes, addresses or birthdays. Please use first names or roles (Mom, Dad) rather than full names for children.

How we use it

Only to make and update your plan, send you its link, answer your questions, and keep the service working. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. If you sign up for our emails separately, that list is managed in Flodesk and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who helps us

A few service providers process information for us to run the service: Stripe (payments), Render (hosting), Resend (email), and the software provider that helps generate your plan from your answers. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

Your plan page

Your plan is at a private link that isn't listed in search engines. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it only with people you choose.

How long we keep it

We keep your answers and plan for 12 months after your photo date so your link keeps working, then delete them. Ask us any time and we'll delete them sooner.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete your information, or to take down your plan page.

Children

Plans are bought by adults. The only details about children are the first names, age groups and preferences a parent chooses to share, used only to style that family's plan.
