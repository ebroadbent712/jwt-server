Just Wear This.

Get set up

About 10 minutes, once. After that, every client's outfit plan takes a couple of minutes.

You'll need a Claude account (the free plan works) and the personal connector link Emily sent you.

1. Turn on code execution

In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, and turn on Code execution and file creation. Just Wear This uses it to lay out each family's board.

2. Install the plugin

  1. Open Customize, then Plugins.
  2. Choose Add marketplace and type ps-tools-store/dhxp-9pkw
  3. Find Just Wear This and tap Install.
  4. Turn on Sync automatically, so new pieces and fixes arrive on their own.

3. Connect your client pages

This is what turns each plan into a link your client opens on her phone.

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Just Wear This.
  3. Paste your personal connector link (it starts with justwearthis.co/mcp/) and save.

Your link is just for your business, so please don't share it.

4. Make your first plan

Start a new chat and say:

Just Wear This, make my client questionnaire.

You'll get a link to a short, tap-to-answer questionnaire with your business name on it. Use that one link for every client. When answers come back, paste them into a new chat with what you know about the session:

Plan for the Parkers. Family session, late October, Loose Park under the big trees, golden hour. Here are her answers: …

The first time, Claude asks a few quick questions about your business (your editing style, how clients reach you, colors you never want) and suggests one line to save in your Claude profile so it never asks again.

5. Send it

You'll get a justwearthis.co/p/… link. Text or email it to your client. She opens it on her phone, no login: everyone's outfit, the colors and why they work, every piece to shop, and buttons to mark what she has or has ordered. Ask for a change any time and the same link updates.

Things you can say

"Warmer layer for Dad" or "No pattern on the baby"Swaps just that piece and updates the page.
Send a photo: "Does this work?"A yes, no or "yes if," with the reason, and an offer to build around it.
"Mom already has a cream sweater she loves"Builds the plan around it and marks it "You have this."
"Make a plan for the Lees: Sarah, Mike, Ava 6, baby Theo, Loose Park in October"A surprise plan with no questionnaire.

If something's off

Claude doesn't seem to know Just Wear This. Start a new chat and begin with "Just Wear This:". Check it's turned on under Customize, Plugins.

You got a file instead of a link. Check the connector from step 3 is added and turned on, then ask Claude to publish the page.

Anything else, or a piece that doesn't look right: text me or email emily@justwearthis.co. While you're testing, that's exactly what I want to hear.
